# A student day in Hyderabad

The example is a fixed Old City route: MGBS → Chowmahalla Palace → Charminar → lunch → Laad Bazaar → MGBS. It demonstrates local research without a complex routing engine. Hotel arrival planning is a separate editable feature.

Sources checked 18 October 2026:
- [Official palace visitor information](https://www.chowmahalla.in/visit/): Saturday–Thursday 10:00–17:00, Friday closed; approximately two hours suggested. Charminar is listed less than 1 km away; MGBS requires onward travel.
- [Hyderabad district tourism](https://hyderabad.telangana.gov.in/tourism/): destination background for further reading.

Only the palace weekday closure is encoded. Temporary closures, holidays, Charminar entry, fares, tickets, walking conditions and market opening times must be checked before travel. Transfer and meal durations are chosen example buffers. The app does not guarantee services or prices.

Explain it in an interview: “I kept one route small and source-backed, then used a weekday condition to avoid recommending a closed palace. I would need current provider data before making that check comprehensive.”
